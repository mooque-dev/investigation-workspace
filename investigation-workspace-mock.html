How this assistant works

It investigates alongside you — doing the legwork fast and bringing you in at the moments that matter. Here's exactly what it will and won't do on its own.

Why onboarding at all? In a regulated plant, trust has to be calibrated before the first alarm, not during one. These four rungs are the whole interaction model — surfaced up front so the user is never surprised by what the AI does autonomously.

DOES SILENTLY
Reads data & runs read-only checks
Pulls sensor history, validates the signal, scans recent events. Nothing here can change the batch, so it just happens — instantly — the moment you arrive.
SHOWS ITS WORK
Forms theories — with evidence & confidence
Every hypothesis is ranked, with what supports it, what argues against it, and how sure it is. It never gives you one confident answer.
YOU SIGN OFF
Drafts records, escalations & heavier analysis
It prepares the official deviation note or escalation — you review and approve before anything is committed or sent.
YOU ACT — NOT IT
Never touches the reactor
It can recommend a setpoint change, but you make it yourself in your control system. The AI has no write access to equipment, by design.
DIR A Guided plan + live case file
The AI runs a visible investigation plan with status on every step — done, running, needs-you — and a case file builds itself as an audit trail. This is the "agentic, not a chat box" direction.
